# Limitations

Honest limits of AIC Core v1 — for actuarial readers and judges.

1. **Illustrative parameters** — Class rates, occupation scores, and loadings are Phase-1 demonstrators, not IPEC-filed tariffs.
2. **Charged-premium GLM heritage** — Live Gamma GLM artifacts (when trained) fit price proxies; they are not yet a full loss-cost indicated rate system for motor.
3. **CT Flex Income focus** — Health/Life underwrite still falls back to the legacy product slice; AKL environmental features are scaffolds.
4. **Synthetic / thin histories** — Transaction-count-only quotes synthesize flat observations; real EcoCash streams will change feature distributions.
5. **No claims triangle in the quote path** — Reserving is implemented and validated algebraically, but not yet fed live CT Flex portfolio data.
6. **Validation ≠ certification** — The research validation suite proves internal consistency and designed behaviour; it is not a peer-reviewed empirical study on Zimbabwe gig portfolios.
7. **PAYG mapping** — Decision Engine maps indicated commercial premium to a % of earnings proxy; trip-level fare economics remain a product design choice.
8. **Single credibility strategy in production** — Bühlmann–Straub only; Jewell/Bayesian are placeholders.

These limitations are intentional for a research / competition platform and should be cited in any paper or technical report.
